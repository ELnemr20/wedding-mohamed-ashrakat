# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Wedding guests viewing the invitation on a phone or desktop browser.

## Product Purpose

Present Mohamed and Ashrakat's wedding invitation, essential event details, venue directions, and a countdown in one shareable page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static single-page HTML invitation with an interactive opening action.
- Confirmed details include 29 September 2026 and Ramaj Hotel in Fifth Settlement, New Cairo, Egypt.
- The ceremony time is not yet available and must remain clearly marked as to be announced.
- The page must work on phones and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Brand Commitments

- Couple: Mohamed and Ashrakat.
- Formal, warm wedding-invitation voice in English with a short Arabic welcome line.
- The supplied visual reference establishes ivory flowers, navy foliage, champagne-gold detail, and a premium stationery mood as the desired direction.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing invitation copy and implementation: `wedding-mohamed-ashrakat.html`.
- User-supplied reference images in this conversation.
- Project floral asset: `assets/floral-corner.png`.

## Product Principles

- Make the event's key details effortless to find.
- Let the invitation feel ceremonial without delaying access to information.
- Keep every decorative choice in service of the couple and event.
