# Design System

## Visual Direction

An ivory wedding stationery composition with substantial botanical artwork growing inward from the page edges. Deep navy provides formal structure; champagne gold is used for fine borders and ornamental detail. The hero is a flat invitation panel, never a dome or pointed arch.

## Typography

- Display: Playfair Display for the couple's names and principal headings.
- Supporting copy: Cormorant Garamond with restrained uppercase tracking.
- Accent: Parisienne only for the conjunction between the names.

## Layout

- Mobile-first, vertically composed invitation panel with generous pale negative space.
- Florals anchor opposite sides of the hero and stay behind readable content.
- A thin double gold frame creates hierarchy without heavy line work.

## Motion

- The only authored focal sequence is the invitation opening.
- On activation, real floral artwork enters from the two sides, followed by the details scroll.
- Reduced-motion users move directly to the details.

## Surface Rules

- Use the navy, gold, ivory, and cream tokens already defined in the page.
- Decorative botanical art is real raster artwork with transparent backgrounds, not CSS petal substitutes.
- Keep controls direct, highly visible, and keyboard accessible.
